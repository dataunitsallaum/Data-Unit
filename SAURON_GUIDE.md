# Data & AI Unit dashboard

## What it is for
The Data & AI Unit dashboard is a one-page overview of the unit's work. It maps every project the unit has delivered and every project it plans. It covers Sallaum Lines, Sallaum Medical Center (SMC) and the Sallaum Humanity Foundation. Management uses it to see what exists, what is new and what comes next. It also works as a launch pad. Many projects on it open their live Power BI report or app in one click. The page holds no live data. The unit writes every figure and list by hand. They change only when the unit publishes an update.

## Who uses it
- Sallaum management use it to follow what the Data & AI Unit delivers and plans.
- The Data & AI Unit prepares and updates it. The page names Wael Frashy, Lead, Data & AI Unit, as its author.
- Anyone with a Sallaum Microsoft account can open it.
- It is not on the Sallaum Enterprises portal list. Sauron answers questions about it for portal admins only.

## Getting in
1. Open https://nice-sea-0604a7a10.7.azurestaticapps.net in your browser.
2. A "Signing you in…" screen appears. It sends you to the Microsoft sign-in page.
3. Sign in with your Sallaum Microsoft account. Personal accounts and other companies' accounts are refused.
4. The dashboard opens after sign-in.

To sign out, click "Sign out" in the dark footer at the bottom of the page.

If sign-in fails, the screen shows "Sign-in failed" and a "Try again" button. It can also say it could not load the Microsoft sign-in library. In both cases, click "Try again" once. If it fails again, contact the Data & AI Unit.

A second copy of the page lives at https://data-ai-unit.netlify.app. The unit updates that copy by hand. It can be older than the main site.

Getting into the dashboard does not open the linked reports and apps for you. Each one has its own sign-in and its own access list.

## Page: AI Operating Model (hidden)
The AI Operating Model is a second page inside the dashboard. Its menu link is switched off. To see it, add #aimodel to the end of the web address. "Back to overview" returns to the main page.

It explains three things:
- **How the agents work:** every agent follows four steps. A trigger starts it, such as a question, an email, a schedule or new data. The agent then retrieves the facts, reasons about them and acts.
- **Our work feeds the agents:** agents built on Claude and Copilot look up facts in the unit's Power BI, SharePoint and OneDrive data. This look-up step is called retrieval, or RAG.
- **Data classification:** four levels decide which data may go into which AI tool.
  - Confidential (personal, financial, health, legal, unpublished contracts): never in outside AI tools.
  - Restricted (internal KPIs, client names, vessel schedules, pricing, HR): approved internal AI only.
  - Internal (summaries, anonymized trends, process documents): usable with AI after removing names and identifiers.
  - Public (published reports, public company and industry facts): usable with any AI tool.

## What the numbers mean
- **Organizations served (3):** Sallaum Lines, Sallaum Medical Center and the Sallaum Humanity Foundation.
- **Departments covered (8):** a fixed figure written by the unit.
- **Solutions delivered (30+):** a fixed figure written by the unit. It is not a count of the dots on the map.
- **Hours per month saved (450+):** a fixed figure. It matches the sum of the "Est. saved" figures on the delivery cards (100 + 200 + 20 + 12 + 15 + 100 + 10 = 457). These are the unit's estimates. They are not measured.
- **AI initiatives live 2026 (6+):** a fixed figure written by the unit.
- **Reporting turnaround (Days → min):** reports that used to take days now take minutes.
- **Support hrs/mo:** the monthly support hours the card lists for that area. Only four cards show one.
- **Est. saved hrs/mo:** staff hours the unit estimates it saves that area each month.
- **Card badge (for example "4 months"):** the delivery timeline for that area.
- **Update date:** the day the unit added the item to the dashboard. It is not always the go-live date.
- **Dots:** solid means delivered or in progress. Hollow and dashed means planned. A ring with ↗ opens a link. A glow means the project is in Latest updates.
- **Bell number:** how many items were added since you last opened the bell. It is kept in this browser only.

## Common questions
**Is the data on this dashboard live?**
No. The unit writes every figure and list by hand. Nothing refreshes on its own.

**How is the 450+ hours saved worked out?**
It is a fixed figure. It matches the total of the estimated monthly hours saved on the delivery cards, which is 457 hours. The figures are estimates, not measurements.

**Why can I not open a report from the map?**
The dashboard only links to it. The report or app checks your access separately. Ask its owner for access.

**Why does the SMC Module link refuse me?**
That report sits in a different Microsoft tenant from the other links. A Sallaum account may not have access there.

**Why are the menu links missing on my phone?**
The menu hides its links on narrow screens. Scroll down to reach each section.

**Why did the bell not show a new number?**
The bell counts how many items the feed holds, not which ones. The feed holds nine at most. Once it is full, a new item replaces the oldest and the count stays the same. Check the dates on the Latest updates cards instead.

**Where is the AI Model page?**
Its menu link is switched off. Add #aimodel to the end of the web address to open it.

**How do I get something added or changed?**
Ask the Data & AI Unit. The unit updates and publishes the page on request.

**The page looks out of date after a change. What do I do?**
Press Ctrl+Shift+R to reload it fully.

## Limits
- The dashboard holds no live data. Figures, lists and dates change only when the unit publishes an update.
- Latest updates shows the nine newest items only. Older items drop off.
- Many dots have no link. Agents, for example, have no page to open.
- Every linked report and app needs its own access.
- The Netlify copy is updated by hand and can lag behind the main site.
- The menu links are hidden on phones and narrow windows.
- The footer says "Generated June 2026". This is fixed text, not the last update date.
- The "R&D New Ventures" delivery card uses an older name. The same products now sit in the Tech Hold pillar.
- The page has no search and no export.
